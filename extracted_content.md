## Discover Chesterfield Community Church

Imagine a space where God's family makes room for you   
to connect in friendship and explore your  
deepest faith questions. 

We invite you to experience such fresh faith  with us here at Chesterfield Community Church.

### **Sunday**

### **9:30am - Small Group Bible Study**

### **10:30am - Worship**

### [**In-person and on-line at You Tube (click here for link)**](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=chesterfield+community+church+of+god+chesterfield+indiana)

_When you join us for worship on a Sunday morning, you will find our service includes an uplifting musical worship and a Biblical message for daily living. A nursery and children's classes are  provided during Sunday worship._

Located on the corner of Main Street and Linden Lane in Chesterfield.
